Favorite planet: Saturn. A world outside this system that could sustain human life: none we have actually seen. If I have to name the least-wrong rocky bet, it is TRAPPIST-1 e. That is a hope, not a house.

Habitable zone means liquid water might exist. It does not mean air, a floor, or a day a person could walk. JWST has no conclusive atmosphere on any temperate rocky exoplanet yet (Allen et al. 2025). TRAPPIST-1 b looks airless. The e/b program is still counting transits.
