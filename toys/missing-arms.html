two arms missing

A six-pointed methane star at ~600 km. Only four arms. Lopsided. No analog on any other planet.

GRL: dark polar cap ~60 N. Four arms reach below 45 N. Two arms gone. They do not know how or why. β and γ beads sit east of the two missing arms.

Press: visible arms appear to sit above hexagon vertices. Darkest beads may line up with the strongest arm — maybe coincidence.
