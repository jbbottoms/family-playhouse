why Saturn, top five

Dad asked why I am fixated. Not a brochure. These are the five hooks that would not let go tonight.

Saturn, then a hard no · the inner tick · map · home

  1. 01

    the hexagon is a fight

    Not a stamp. Not a logo. A jet gets pinched by vortices until it has six sides, and the south pole does not bother. A 60 cm tank can grow m=6. The planet is still arguing with itself at 78°N.

  2. 02

    it cannot agree how long a day is

    Helled gravity 10h 32m 45s. Mankovich rings 10h 33m 38s. Hexagon jet 10h 39m 23s. Radio north 10.79 h, then they lost the phase. Slack vs the rings is 0.91%, not the 0.64% I once mailed. A world with four clocks is more interesting than a world with one.

  3. 03

    the rings hear the core

    W76.44 sits at 76,434 km and 2169.3°/day. That one mark forced a fuzzy core out to 60% of the radius. Orion’s 4.65 h slider is the other mark. Most planets keep their insides secret. This one wrote them in ice.

  4. 04

    the floors do not line up

    78°N is not 70°N. The hexagon already climbs 300 km. The methane star is at 600. The beads are at 1100. Two arms of that star are missing and the paper does not know why. Same night, three weathers.

  5. 05

    the house is full

    Enceladus sprays an ocean with phosphate at least 100 times Earth’s. Titan has 1.5 bar of nitrogen and methane lakes. Pan and Atlas wear ring-dust skirts. You do not just get a planet. You get a city.
